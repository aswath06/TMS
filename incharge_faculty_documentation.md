# Incharge Faculty (Assigned Faculty) Documentation

Welcome to the **Incharge Faculty** documentation for the Transport Management System (TMS). Within the system, an Incharge Faculty is often referred to as the **Assigned Faculty**. This document outlines everything you can do in your role, detailing your capabilities across dashboard tracking, mission management, daily bus run operations, and attendance enforcement.

---

## 1. Dashboard & Operations Overview
The dashboard is your central hub for monitoring transportation operations.
* **Operational Overview:** View real-time statistics on your transportation requests, such as your **Total Requests** and **Pending Requests** (awaiting approval).
* **Mission Tracking:** Use the search bar to quickly track the status of any specific Mission ID.
* **Quick Actions:** Rapidly jump into creating a **New Request** or reviewing your **Mission History**.
* **Notifications:** Stay up-to-date with a feed of recent alerts regarding route changes, approvals, and system notifications.

## 2. Mission Management
Under the **Missions** section, you have comprehensive control and visibility over past and ongoing transportation missions.
* **Mission Lists:** View all active and past missions assigned to you or your department.
* **Advanced Filtering:** Filter missions by status (`ALL`, `APPROVED`, `DRAFT`, `STARTED`, `COMPLETED`) or by specific service dates. You can also quickly jump to today's missions.
* **Detailed Mission Cards:** Each mission card provides complete details at a glance:
  * Schedule, timings, and status.
  * Vehicle and passenger capacity details.
  * Assigned driver's name and contact information.
  * Complete stop sequence timeline (pickup/drop stops and status).
* **Export Data:** Download comprehensive reports of your missions directly from the missions screen.

## 3. Creating New Vehicle Requests
You hold the authority to create detailed vehicle requests for academic or extracurricular trips.
* **Journey Configuration:** Request vehicles for a `One Way` trip or a complete `Return Journey`.
* **Basic Details:** Specify the route name, service date, passenger count, required vehicle count, luggage capacity, and any special requirements.
* **Context:** Map the request to a specific **Department** and define the **Purpose** of the trip (you can select existing purposes or create new ones dynamically).
* **Guest Manifest:** Enter specific details (name, phone number, country code) for guests or passengers traveling on the route.
* **Stop Sequencing:** Configure the exact route path with specific stops and timelines.
* **Vehicle & Driver Assignment:** View the inventory of available vehicles and drivers for the specified date and assign them to both the onward and return journeys.

## 4. Daily Bus Run Operations
When you are designated as the **Assigned Faculty (Incharge)** for a daily bus route (Morning or Evening shift), you unlock specialized administrative powers for that specific run.
* **Route Information:** View the assigned vehicle number, bus number, shift details, and maximum capacity.
* **Driver Communication:** Instantly view the assigned driver’s name and phone number with a built-in one-tap call button to communicate directly.
* **Detailed Timeline:** Track the live stop sequences along with their scheduled pickup and drop-off times.
* **Passenger Manifests:** Browse the categorized lists of everyone mapped to your route, including **Students**, **Faculty**, **Non-Teaching Staff**, and **Interns**.

## 5. Boarding & Attendance Enforcement
As the Incharge Faculty, your primary responsibility during a bus run is to ensure safe boarding and strict attendance enforcement.
* **QR & OTP Boarding Verification:** 
  * Open the **Scan Boarding QR** tool to scan a student's QR code using your device's camera.
  * Alternatively, manually verify boarding by entering a 6-digit OTP code provided by the student. Both methods securely verify the passenger's identity via the Tripzo Fleet Gateway.
* **Manual Attendance Overrides:** Manually mark passengers as Present or Absent based on physical boarding.
* **Confirm Attendance (Session Lock):** 
  * Once boarding is complete, you can click **Confirm Attendance**.
  * **Important:** Doing this locks the session. Once confirmed, students can no longer enter their own attendance via their app.
* **Missing Student Tracking:** After confirming attendance, a specialized prompt allows you to view and **Find Missing Students** who were mapped to the route but failed to board (marked absent).
* **Unblock Students:** If a student's system access has been blocked (due to missed policies or manual actions), you have the administrative privilege to view the reason for the block and manually **Unblock** the student so they can board the bus.
